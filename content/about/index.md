## Yesterday

The Aidi Group began with operators. Our founder built Termii, a communications platform for businesses across Africa, from the ground up. Building it taught us what it takes to create infrastructure that others rely on every day.

## Today

We build and hold infrastructure companies, back African and diaspora founders, give the diaspora and Nigerians a bridge to global wealth, and host professionals and travellers. We work from San Jose and Lagos.

## Tomorrow

We are building for decades, not quarters. We intend to hold our companies for the long term, grow them with patient capital, and hand a stronger group to the next generation.
