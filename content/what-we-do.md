## Build

We found and own infrastructure companies in communications, AI and payments: [Termii](https://termii.com), [Telroi](https://telroi.ai) and Siu Telecoms (Sotel).

## Back

Through [Aidi Ventures](/back), we invest in exceptional African and diaspora founders building for the world.

## Bridge

Through [Aidi Wealth](https://joinaidi.com), we give the diaspora and Nigerians access to cross-border wealth. Aidi Wealth Nigeria is in preparation.

## Host

Through [Aidi Haven](/host), we operate short-stay homes for professionals, founders and diaspora travellers: San Jose today, with other hubs, including Lagos, planned.

## Protect

We keep the group's control, liquidity and succession secure, so our companies can be held for the long term and passed on to the next generation.
