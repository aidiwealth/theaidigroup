## Our values

**Build what lasts.** We start and run companies that others can depend on.

**Back people early.** We invest in talented founders before the rest of the market sees them.

**Bridge markets.** We connect the US and Africa in both directions: capital, talent and customers.

**Earn trust.** We say what we do, do what we say, and keep our clients' and partners' information private.

**Think in generations.** We make decisions we would be proud to explain to the next generation.

## What we are not

We are not a multi-family office. Aidi Wealth serves its clients separately, under its own regulation. We are not a trading desk: we hold businesses for the long term.
