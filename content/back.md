## What we look for

- Technical founders building products for global customers
- A clear view of the problem, and early proof that customers want the solution
- Teams that can build in hard markets and scale beyond them

We are operators first. Alongside capital, founders get support from people who have built and run infrastructure companies in the US and Africa.
